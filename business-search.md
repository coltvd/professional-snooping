# Business Search

Tools for researching businesses, verifying registrations, and checking reputations.

---

## Business Registration & Filings

| Tool | URL | Notes |
|------|-----|-------|
| BizFile Online (CA SOS) | [bizfileonline.sos.ca.gov](https://bizfileonline.sos.ca.gov/search/business) | California Secretary of State business search |
| Bizapedia | [bizapedia.com](https://www.bizapedia.com) | Nationwide business records, registered agents, officers |

> For other states, search `"[state] Secretary of State business search"` — all states have a public business registry.

---

## Reputation & Complaints

| Tool | URL | Notes |
|------|-----|-------|
| Better Business Bureau | [bbb.org](https://www.bbb.org) | Complaints, ratings, accreditation status |

---

## Tips

- Cross-reference the registered agent on filings — it often reveals the true owner of a shell company
- Check for DBAs (Doing Business As) under the same owner
- Look up officers/directors by name to find all entities they're connected to
- Use [LinkedIn](https://linkedin.com) to verify claimed employment history against public profiles

## Identifying Fake Profile Photos

Signs an image may be AI-generated:
- Asymmetrical earrings or jewelry
- Blurry or melted background elements
- Unnatural hair at the edges/boundary
- Teeth misalignment or extra fingers
- Artifacts in glasses reflections

Run suspected fake photos through Sensity or a reverse image search ([Google OSINT](google-osint.md)) — AI-generated images won't return matches, but stolen real photos will.
